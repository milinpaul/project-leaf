# Code of Conduct

Leaf is built in the open, and we want everyone who takes part to have a good time doing it. This applies to
issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and anywhere else the project is represented.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. People join from many backgrounds and with many levels of experience.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Critique ideas and code, never people. Explain the *why* behind feedback.
- Accept a decision gracefully once it is made, even if you argued for another one.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or other conduct a reasonable person would consider disruptive.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer
[@milinpaul](https://github.com/milinpaul) privately, or use GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature. Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may
warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful to the community.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
