## Goal

A task block for things to do by a date, different from the plain to-do checkbox: text, a due date with an
optional time, a priority (High, Medium, Low or none), and a way to tick it off. It looks different from a to-do
(a round check, the date and a priority flag beside the text) and its date turns red once it is overdue and
not done. Asked for by the owner (2026-10-30) and added to Phase 0.

## Notes

- Owner's choices: priority is High / Medium / Low / none; the only extra is the overdue highlight (no
  reminders, no notes field: children nest under a task as under a list item); a list of tasks across pages comes
  later, in E20 (Phase 1).
- A due date is a calendar date, with an optional wall-clock time, in the owner's own time: stored as `2026-10-30`
  and `14:30`, never converted between time zones, so a task due on Friday stays due on Friday when travelling.
- The editor never reads the clock (as with page edited times): "today" comes from the app, so overdue is testable.
