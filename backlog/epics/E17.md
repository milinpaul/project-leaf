## Goal

Phase 1. A thin bar across the top of every open page, like Notion's: on the left where the page sits (its parent
pages, then its own icon and title); on the right when it was last edited ("Edited just now", "Edited 3 minutes
ago", "Edited 100 days ago"), a star to add it to Favourites, and a `⋯` menu with the page's actions (rename, move
to trash, and more later). Notion's Share, link and privacy controls are left out: Leaf is local and single-user
until sync.

## Notes

Agreed with the owner (2026-10-30), from a Notion screenshot:

- **Keep:** the page's place (breadcrumb), the edited time, the star, the `⋯` menu.
- **Leave out:** Share, copy link, the Private/Shared badge and comments. They need sync or links (E10).
- **Edited time** is a new fact about each page: when its blocks, title, icon or cover last changed, kept in the
  workspace document so it survives restarts and will sync. Shown relative and kept current while the page is open;
  the exact date and time on hover.
- **Favourites:** starring adds the page to a "Favourites" section at the top of the sidebar; it is per workspace
  and saved with it. Trashing a page takes it out; restoring brings it back.
- **The `⋯` menu is built to grow:** its items come from one list in `leaf-editor`, so later stories (duplicate,
  move to, export, page width, version history) add a line rather than a new menu. At first: Add to / Remove from
  Favourites, Rename, Move to Trash, and a footer with the created and edited times.
- **Same actions everywhere:** the sidebar's right-click menu and the `⋯` menu run the same commands.

Order: S01 edited time model and S02 favourites model (in parallel) → S03 top bar → S04 `⋯` menu, S05
favourites in the sidebar (in parallel).
