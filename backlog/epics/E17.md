## Goal

One place to track bugs the owner (or an agent) finds in the app, so they're easy to see and count on the board.

## Notes

- A bug story is titled by what should happen ("Arrow keys in the emoji picker"), says in Context what goes wrong
  and where in the code, and carries the `bug` label plus the crate labels it touches.
- Every fix starts with a failing test (CLAUDE.md).
- Stays in the current phase: move it with `backlog.py phase E17 <n>` when Phase 0 is done.
- Bugs filed before this epic existed stay in their original epics (e.g. E08-S06).
