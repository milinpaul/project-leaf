## Goal

More than one way to look at a database: a board view that groups rows into columns by a property (drag a card to
change its value), and saved views shown as tabs above the database, each with its own layout, sort, filters and
shown properties.

## Notes

Data model in ADR 0075 (spike E22-S01). The owner moved board views and saved views into Phase 1, after the rest of
E09 (2026-10-31). Boards group by a select column for now; board columns follow the option order; every view keeps
all its own settings.
