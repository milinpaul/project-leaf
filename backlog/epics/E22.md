## Goal

More than one way to look at a database: a board view that groups rows into columns by a property (drag a card to
change its value), and saved views shown as tabs above the database, each with its own layout, sort, filters and
shown properties.

## Notes

The spec lists board views under Phase 2. The owner asked for a spike (2026-10-31); E22-S01 proposes the data model
(ADR 0075) and the stories that follow from it.
