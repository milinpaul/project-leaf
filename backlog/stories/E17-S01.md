## Context

The page scrolls in jumps instead of smoothly, unlike a web page. Scrolling happens in the virtual page list
(`crates/leaf-ui/src/shell/page_list.rs`, ADR 0035). Find out whether it comes from wheel events being turned
into whole lines, a re-layout that moves the content (rows changing height as they are measured), or redraw cost,
and fix the cause. Check trackpad (pixel deltas, momentum) and mouse wheel (line deltas) on macOS.

- [ ] Holds on the 10,000-block fixture page (`just fixture`) within the frame budget
- [ ] Cause and fix recorded in the story Log (ADR if the approach changes)
