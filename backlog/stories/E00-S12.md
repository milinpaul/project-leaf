## Context

ADRs were named `NNNN-title.md`, and agents working in parallel picked the same next number (0061, 0063 and 0074
clashed), causing merge conflicts and renames. The owner wants the order kept and the story in the name, for every
ADR, old and new (2026-10-31): `ADR-001-E08-S08-bundled-code-font.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every ADR is renamed `ADR-NNN-<story>-<title>.md` (three-digit order number, the first story it records or its
      epic, then the old title part; none when it has no story), and its heading reads `# ADR-NNN. Title`
- [x] Every link and mention in code, docs and stories follows (`ADR 0068` → `ADR-068`); the index lists them in
      order with the new names; the template, CLAUDE.md and `docs/README.md` describe the new naming

## Plan

- A one-off script renames every `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md` to `ADR-NNN-<story>-<title>.md` (the first story on its
  Story line, its epic if it spans one, nothing if none) and rewrites links and mentions everywhere
  (`ADR 0068` to `ADR-068`, `ADRs 0001-0005` to `ADR-001 to ADR-005`).
- Template, index instructions, CLAUDE.md and `docs/README.md` describe the new naming. A CI check for duplicate
  numbers was tried and dropped at the owner's call: it wouldn't remove the conflicts, only report them.
