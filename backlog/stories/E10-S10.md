## Context

The owner asked (2026-10-31) for a way to link selected text to a page from the floating formatting bar, with an
icon, like Notion's "Link to page". The owner chose: a new button with a page icon next to Link (which stays for web
addresses); it opens the same page list as `[[` with the selected words as the search, and the chosen page's link
replaces the words (drawn with its icon and title, ADR-071).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The floating bar shows a "Link to page" button with a page icon, after Link, when the selection is text in one block that takes links (not code) without a line break or `]`
- [x] Clicking it opens the page list under the selection, searching for the selected words; Enter or a click swaps the words for a link to the chosen page (or "New page" with those words as its title)
- [x] Escape gives the selected words back as they were (nothing changes); undo after linking gives them back too
- [x] Tests (editor and window); screenshots light and dark; `just check` passes

## Plan

- `FormatBar::link_to_page: bool`, offered for a one-block text selection that can hold a link and reads as a query.
- `PageEditor::link_selection_to_page()`: replaces the selection with `[[` + its words (a step of its own) and opens
  the `[[` menu, remembering it came from a selection; `link_menu_escape()` undoes that step for such a menu, else
  closes it as before. Choosing works exactly like the `[[` menu, so the words and `[[` go and the link takes their
  place.
- UI: a button with the `FileText` icon in `shell/format_bar.rs`; Escape calls `link_menu_escape`.
