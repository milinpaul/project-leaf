## Context

The owner asked (2026-11-01) for Notion's hover preview: resting the pointer on a link to a page shows a small card
with that page's content, so you can see it is the right page before opening it. Notion added it in October 2023;
many people found it got in the way on pages full of links and asked for a way to turn it off. The owner accepted
these choices (2026-11-01):

- the card appears after about 0.6 s on a link, never while typing, dragging or selecting;
- it goes when the pointer leaves both the link and the card, on a key press, on a click and on scrolling; Esc
  closes it;
- it sits below the line, or above it near the window's bottom, so it never covers the link's line;
- it shows the page's place in the page tree, its icon and title, then its first few blocks as small text;
- for a link to a block, the linked block (highlighted) and the blocks around it;
- a page in the trash shows its title and "In trash"; a deleted page shows no card;
- web links are left out (previews of websites go online, ADR-080);
- Settings has a switch to turn the previews off.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Resting on a page link shows the card after a short pause; moving off the link and the card hides it
- [x] The card shows the page's path, icon and title, and its first blocks (headings, lists, to-dos, quotes, code, …)
- [x] A link to a block shows that block highlighted, with the blocks around it
- [x] A page in the trash: title and "In trash"; a deleted page: no card
- [x] A key press, a click, scrolling or Esc hides it; it never opens while a mouse button is down
- [x] A click on the card opens the page (at the block, for a link to one)
- [x] Settings › Appearance has a switch to turn previews off, remembered across launches
- [x] Tests (editor and window); `just check` passes

## Plan

- **`leaf-editor`**, `workspace_editor/page_preview.rs`: `WorkspaceEditor::page_preview(page, block)` gives a
  `PagePreview` (path, icon, title, live or in trash, up to `PREVIEW_LINES` lines in reading order, whether there is
  more). Each `PreviewLine` has a kind (text, heading, bullet, number, to-do, quote, code, …), plain words (links as
  their titles), nesting depth and whether it is the linked block. `None` for a deleted page. A page not loaded
  yet says so, and the UI loads it from disk when the pause ends.
- **`leaf-store`**: `AppSettings::link_previews()` / `set_link_previews()` (on unless turned off, kept on save).
- **`leaf-ui`**, `shell/link_preview.rs`: the row's mouse move asks the painted layout which link is under the
  pointer (`PaintedLayout::link_under`, with where its line is); a 600 ms timer then builds the preview and places
  the card (`slash_popover::place`: below, or above). Leaving the row or the card starts a short grace timer so the
  pointer can cross to the card. Key press (capture on the page), mouse down, a scroll (list offset changed) and Esc
  hide it. A click on the card calls `open_at`. Settings › Appearance gets the switch.
- **Tests:** editor unit tests for the preview (kinds, order, numbering, cut-off, links as titles, block window,
  trash, missing, unloaded); settings round trip; window tests for show after the pause, hide on leaving, key, Esc,
  the switch, and the click opening the page. Screenshot of the card.
- **Docs:** ARCHITECTURE.md (link previews), PRODUCT_SPEC.md (page links), ADR-XXX-E10-S12.
