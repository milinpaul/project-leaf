## Context

The owner finds it hard to tell where they are in a long page: there is no scrollbar (2026-10-31). They want one
that is slim, modern and calm, in keeping with ADR-027's look.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The page has a slim vertical scrollbar at its right edge: a rounded pill with no track, faint while idle so the
      position can always be read, a little wider and darker on hover and while dragged
- [x] Dragging the pill scrolls the page and clicking the track jumps there; the wheel and trackpad work as before
- [x] The sidebar page tree gets the same scrollbar; nothing shows when everything fits
- [x] Light and dark both look right (screenshots); window tests; `just check` passes

## Plan

- `leaf-ui/src/scrollbar.rs`: `slim(handle)` builds gpui-component's `Scrollbar::vertical` with Leaf's styles
  (theme colours, rounded thumb, no track, always shown) so every scrolling area shares one look.
- Overlay it on the page's list (`shell/blocks.rs`) and the sidebar tree (`shell/tree.rs`).
- Window test: the bar is drawn on a long page; dragging its thumb moves the page. Screenshots light and dark.
